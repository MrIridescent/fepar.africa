Priorities, not claims

Development that keeps people, culture and capability in view.

Our focus areas are priorities for partnership and exploration — not claims of completed projects. We invite serious collaborators to help shape what comes next.

Focus areas

Where we are
looking to learn and build.

Each priority connects to the wider FEPAR proposition: agency, dignity, opportunity and durable systems.

01

Youth development

Support young Africans with pathways to skills, confidence, meaningful work and leadership.

02

Talent & enterprise

Connect creative and entrepreneurial talent to mentorship, markets, resources and peer networks.

03

Cultural rights

Advance the recognition, protection and responsible circulation of African cultural knowledge and work.

04

Infrastructure & industry

Explore the practical foundations — spaces, systems, policy and partnerships — that enable industries to thrive.

05

Diaspora connection

Turn global relationships into reciprocal learning, investment and institution-building.

06

Research & learning

Make room for evidence, lived experience and honest reflection before scaling an idea.
